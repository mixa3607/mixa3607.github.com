### Takeaways

- **Generation** is faster than 6.3.3 on every tested 7.x/10.x version. Per-model median gains are **+19…+29%** for the dense Qwen3.8-27B and Gemma 31B models, **+16…+20%** for MoE Gemma 26B, and **+6…+9%** for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next. Newer ROCm does not automatically mean faster generation.
- **Prompt processing on 4 GPUs (tensor)** benefits most from 7.14/10.0/10.1: median gains versus 6.3.3 are **+9…+20%** for Qwen3.8-27B, **+18…+19%** for Gemma 26B, and **+15…+16%** for Gemma 31B. The winner depends on model and context; 7.2.4 is much closer to the baseline.
- **Prompt processing on 2 GPUs (tensor)** is mixed. On 7.14/10.0/10.1, Qwen3.8-27B gains **+3…+20%**, Gemma 26B ranges from **−0.5% to +10.5%**, and Gemma 31B from **−3.4% to +5.7%**, depending on depth. 7.2.4 regresses on Gemma 26B (**−2…−8%**) and Gemma 31B (**−7…−14%**).
- **Single-GPU prompt processing (layer, Gemma 26B)** regresses at 32K/64K context: **−2…−7%** on 7.14/10.0/10.1 and **−9…−11%** on 7.2.4. At depth 0, 7.14/10.0/10.1 gain **+5…+7%**; at 16K they remain within about **±1.5%** of 6.3.3.
- **Gains generally shrink with context depth.** Across all models and both tests, median gains versus 6.3.3 fall from **+18…+20%** at depth 0 to **+14…+15%** at 64K for 7.14/10.0/10.1; 7.2.4 drops from **+10%** to **+5%**.
- **7.14, 10.0 and 10.1 are close overall**, while 7.2.4 is weakest for prompt processing. Across the same 32 prompt-processing and 32 generation configurations, 10.1's geometric mean of paired tok/s ratios is **−1.34% / −1.18%** versus 10.0 and **−0.95% / −1.72%** versus 7.14. Qwen Flash prompt processing is a notable exception: its median gain versus 6.3.3 is only **+1.1%** on 10.1, compared with **+8.6…+8.9%** on 10.0/7.14. These are separate runs, not contemporaneous controls; small differences should not be overinterpreted.

## Bench

The command is rendered from the YAML profiles by
[`build-bench-command.py`](https://github.com/mixa3607/ML-gfx906/tree/master/llama.cpp/build-context/extra/bench-profiles),
copied to the container together with the profiles. `ROCM_VER` selects the result directory,
so one loop over the presets covers all ROCm versions. Each version has 64 GPU measurements,
using the same model revisions, split modes, device order and context depths.
Gemma 31B uses the snapshot
`c1ac76e99d5513b141e8adde7288b85c3f9c32ec/gemma-4-31B-it-UD-Q8_K_XL.gguf`
in the shared Hugging Face cache; on 10.1 it was selected directly with `--model` instead of resolving `--hf-repo`.
